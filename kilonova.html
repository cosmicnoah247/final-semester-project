Kilonova Physics

A kilonova is the explosive merger of two neutron stars or a neutron star with a black hole. The collision ejects neutron-rich matter that creates heavy elements through rapid neutron capture.

Kilonovae produce bright optical and infrared emission that fades over days. They are also powerful sources of gravitational waves detected by observatories on Earth.

These mergers enrich galaxies with gold, platinum, and other rare heavy elements. The process helps explain the chemical evolution of the universe and where the heaviest elements come from.

Kilonova observations connect extreme physics, nuclear astrophysics, and multi-messenger astronomy. The event is a bridge between stellar death and the formation of new matter in the cosmos.
